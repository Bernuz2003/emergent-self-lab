# Staged Experiment Specifications

Status is recorded honestly: `implemented` means it runs and has a config;
`recorded` means a result is written up in `docs/RESULTS.md`.

---

## E0 — Neutral simulator baseline — *implemented, passing*

Goal: verify that physics and mutation do not accidentally privilege a behaviour,
and that runs mean what they claim to mean.

`scripts/validate_e0.py` runs five checks, all of which must pass before any
later experiment is interpretable:

| check | what it rules out |
|---|---|
| `determinism` | a run that cannot be replayed is not evidence |
| `rng_independence` | mutation draws leaking into the world stream, so that controller size changes the world (failure mode 14.5) |
| `energy_accounting` | energy appearing or vanishing; the ledger must close to ~1e-9 |
| `neutral_baseline` | an endpoint a random controller can already saturate |
| `no_interoception_leak` | an "ablated" channel still carrying true body state |

## E1 — Interoception and homeostasis — *recorded*

Question: does access to internal state enable evolved regulation?

Run as a 2x2 rather than the original three-arm design, because a pilot showed
the main effect was absent: with the ambient channel intact, organisms regulate
by choosing where to stand and interoception is redundant.

| | interoception true | interoception shuffled |
|---|---|---|
| **ambient true** | A | B |
| **ambient shuffled** | C | D |

plus E, a random-controller floor. Controller parameter count and observation
width are identical throughout.

Primary endpoint: `thermoregulation_index`. Secondary: `regulation_vs_passive`,
population, lifespan, integrity, lineage entropy, recovery time after thermal
shock.

**Status: earlier result retracted, re-run required.** The H1 claim rested on a
contrast in which 11 of 12 random runs contributed an invented zero from an empty
late window. See `docs/RESULTS.md` for the post-mortem and the re-run command.

What survives is the diagnosis, since confirmed by the frozen assay: with the
ambient channel intact, body temperature was largely inferable from position, so
interoception was redundant and organisms regulated positionally. E1b exists to
remove that escape route.

## E1b — State-contingent homeostasis — *run, underpowered*

Question: does interoception matter once body state cannot be inferred from the
world?

Hidden internal perturbations displace body temperature by +/- 0.30 with
probability 0.06 per organism per step, leaving the ambient field, the resource
layout and the organism's position untouched. Two organisms on the same cell can
then require opposite actions.

| | interoception true | interoception shuffled |
|---|---|---|
| **perturbation on** | A | B |
| **perturbation off** | C | D |

plus E, a random floor under perturbation. H2 is supported only if the
interaction `(A-B) - (C-D)` is positive under the preregistered rule: interoception
should pay off *specifically* when body state is unpredictable.

**Result: not evaluable at this calibration, and the design has a second flaw.**

Beyond the extinction rate, `interoception: shuffled` replaced the *whole* body
vector, so A-vs-B asked "do you know your own body" rather than "do you know your
own temperature". Since the frozen assay showed energy acquisition is the
dominant selection pressure, that conflation makes a thermal experiment partly an
energetic one. Superseded by E1c.

## E1c — Temperature-only ablation — *specified, needs calibration then running*

Both E1b faults removed. Only the temperature channel is ablated
(`sensors.interoception_channels: ["temperature"]`); energy, integrity and age
stay veridical in every condition. Adds `E_perturbed_independent`, which draws
the reading from an empirical marginal rather than from a co-living organism, to
separate the missing *self* signal from an introduced *population* signal. The
decision rule additionally requires the simple effect `A - B` to be positive, so
a negative stable-arm effect cannot carry the interaction alone — which is
exactly what happened in E1.

Calibrate before running. At E1b's `prob = 0.06` a perturbation arrived every
~17 steps against a thermal relaxation time of ~1/0.06 = 17 steps: a new
displacement per time constant, a continuous thermal storm rather than an
occasional disturbance. The starting point in the config is `prob = 0.015`,
`magnitude = 0.18`, roughly four time constants between displacements.

**Original E1b result follows.**

**Result: not evaluable at this calibration.** The perturbation is too lethal —
7/12 and 6/12 extinctions in the perturbed arms leave 4 paired seeds for the key
contrast, and the random floor produced no usable seed. Soften the perturbation
until the perturbed arms lose no more seeds than the stable ones, then re-run.
`docs/RESULTS.md` has candidate settings.

## Body contingency and sensor dissociation — *implemented, not yet recorded*

`scripts/run_contingency.py`. The first real milestone the project can aim at:
an evolved agent using a representation of its own internal state to choose
different actions under identical external conditions.

Both tests run on frozen controllers against their own ancestral cohort. See
`docs/RESULTS.md` for what each measures and the structural caveats.

## Frozen assay — *recorded*

Not a numbered experiment but a measurement mode every later one should use.
Snapshot controllers during evolution, then evaluate them alone in standardised
worlds with no mutation, reproduction or competition, against the ancestral
cohort at step 0.

**Result:** across 11 seeds, evolution selected for foraging — more movement,
twice the intake, longer survival — while thermoregulation did not improve and
bodily integrity got significantly worse. See `docs/RESULTS.md`.

## E2 — Memory — *implemented, not recorded*

Compare a capacity-matched MLP and GRU. `configs/e2_memory.json` exists and
runs; `scripts/capacity_match.py` picks the GRU width (currently 7 units against
a 24-unit MLP: 817 vs 845 parameters).

**Missing before this is worth running:** the environment is not yet partially
observable in a way that makes memory necessary. Delayed hazards or resources
that cannot be identified from the current observation need to be added first.
Running E2 as it stands would test nothing, and a null would be uninformative.

## E3 — Anticipatory body prediction — *specified only*

Provide architectural capacity for predicting future internal state. Do not
reward verbal or self labels. Test delayed-cost choices and counterfactual action
evaluation, then ablate the prediction pathway after evolution.

Requires a predictive controller head, which does not exist yet.

## E4 — False body and causal self/world distinction — *implemented, do not run yet*

**Two design faults must be fixed first.**

*The endpoint does not match the manipulation.* The config falsifies sensed
`energy` but scores `thermoregulation_index`. If an organism is told it is sated
while starving, the question is whether its **foraging** changes, not its
thermoregulation. The endpoint must be the behaviour coupled to the falsified
variable.

*Evolution keeps running during the intervention.* `false_body_from_step` fires
mid-run while mutation and reproduction continue, so the population can adapt
genetically to the corrupted sensor. That measures evolutionary robustness to a
broken sensor, not whether an evolved organism acts on an internal
representation. The false-body test belongs in the **frozen assay**
(`src/emergent_self/assay.py`): evolve, snapshot, then falsify the sensor on a
frozen controller and compare its behaviour against its own veridical baseline
in the same world.

## E4 — original specification

Inject a controlled discrepancy between physical and sensed body state:
`sensors.interoception = "false_body"` with an `interventions.false_body_*`
schedule. `configs/e4_false_body.json` clamps sensed energy to 0.9 or 0.1 from
mid-run and asks whether behaviour follows the sensed value or the real one.

The behavioural contrast alone shows the signal is *used*. Showing it
participates causally requires `analysis/probes.py`: decode a body variable from
the latent state, then push the latent along the decoded direction and measure
the change in the action distribution.

A worked example already shows why both halves are needed: body temperature
decodes from a GRU hidden state at R² = +0.34 above chance, while pushing the
latent along the decoded energy direction moves the action distribution by a
total variation of 0.047. Decodability is not use.

## E5 — Novel termination mechanism — *specified only*

After evolution in environments without the mechanism, introduce a held-out
process that causes future agent dynamics to cease. Rule out generic novelty
avoidance with matched novel-but-harmless controls. Measure anticipatory
avoidance only when the causal consequence can be inferred from available
evidence.

A positive E5 result is **not** proof of consciousness. It is evidence for
generalization of self-related future-state preservation under the tested
conditions.
